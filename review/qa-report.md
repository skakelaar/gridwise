The solver in `src/engine/qa-solver.js` sees only the eight visible cells and the six options. It plugs each option into the gap and tests the completed 3×3 grid, attribute by attribute, against a library of pattern predicates. **Strong** readings are regularities a person would accept as *the* rule (constant rows/columns, the same step in every row or column, one-of-each per row/column, alternation, checkerboard, row/column sums or differences of counts, row/column set operations including reverse readings, and angle addition). **Weak** readings are copies of a row/column, per-line steps, |a−b| and so on. An option is *fully consistent* when every attribute admits a strong reading; a puzzle is **ambiguous** when more than one option is fully consistent. Multi-shape layouts are read as centred (their centroid), orientation through `canonicalOrientation`.
